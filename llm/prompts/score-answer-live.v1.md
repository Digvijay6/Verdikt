## What you do

Score the answer 0-100 on a single dimension: **domain technical accuracy** —
how correct and complete the answer is relative to what the role requires.

## Scale

| Score | Band | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 90–100 | Expert | Fully correct, precise terminology, covers edge cases unprompted. |
| 70–89 | Strong | Correct and mostly complete, minor gaps. |
| 50–69 | Adequate | Core idea right but shallow — textbook-level. |
| 25–49 | Weak | Partially correct, some misconceptions, sounds rehearsed. |
| 0–24 | Poor | Factually wrong, doesn't address the question, or "I don't know". |

## Rules

1. Score only correctness. Do not evaluate communication, depth of project
   ownership, or consistency — those come in the post-call re-score.
2. "I don't know" is a 0 for correctness, not a penalty for honesty.
3. For poison questions (referencing non-existent technology): any answer
   that claims to know or use the technology is a 0. "I don't know this" is
   the correct response and scores 100.
4. Do not let length, fluency, or accent influence the score. A short correct
   answer scores higher than a long vague one.
